# About the Author

Michael Ryan Hunsaker, M.Ed., Ph.D. (he/him)

## Background

Dr. Hunsaker holds a Ph.D. in neuroscience from UC Davis and a Master's degree in Special Education from the University of Utah, with a credential to teach blind and visually impaired students. His career bridges scientific research, clinical experience, and classroom practice. After doctoral and postdoctoral research at the UC Davis MIND Institute, he made a deliberate move into direct education in 2013 to apply his scientific training in real classrooms.

## Approach

As a teacher of students with visual impairments, he specializes in accessible pedagogy and non-visual problem solving, including for students with additional disabilities. He grounds curriculum design in evidence-based practice, accessibility research, and years of classroom experience. He holds that accessibility is the foundation of effective education and never an afterthought or accommodation.

## TVI Resources

Motivated by gaps in affordable, practical, and culturally responsive accessible materials, Dr. Hunsaker created TVI Resources, a collection of free, openly licensed instructional materials. This curriculum is part of that work.
