# eBraille Export: Archived Entry

This page preserves the legacy navigation target for eBraille export instruction. The maintained lesson is [Lesson 19: The eBraille Format](../lessons/19-ebraille.md).

That lesson covers the purpose and structure of eBraille, its relationship to BRF and EPUB-compatible packaging, the BrailleBlaster 3.x exporter workflow, experimental-feature cautions, practice activities, and answer guidance. Use it as the current course entry rather than relying on older instructions that may describe a different BrailleBlaster build.

Before using eBraille in production, verify the current DAISY specification, the installed BrailleBlaster version, and the receiving device or reading system's support. Retain BRF and embossed deliverables whenever the project specification requires them; eBraille is a complementary digital format, not a universal replacement.
