## Performance bands

- **90–100 — Strong:** independently executes the workflow, documents decisions, and verifies output with only minor noncritical omissions.
- **80–89 — Competent:** meets essential requirements; any gaps are limited and correctable without compromising the deliverable.
- **70–79 — Developing:** demonstrates partial competence but requires targeted remediation and a successful retest.
- **Below 70 — Not yet competent:** significant workflow or quality gaps require further instruction and reassessment.

A suggested passing threshold is 80/100. An organization may set a different threshold before the assessment; do not change it after seeing the candidate's result.

## Critical-defect override

Regardless of the numerical score, a critical defect prevents an unconditional pass. Examples include unauthorized content loss or substitution, materially incorrect math, missing essential table data, misleading figure treatment, unverifiable source provenance, or a serious known proofreading regression.
