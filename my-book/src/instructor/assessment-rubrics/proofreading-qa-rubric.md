# Proofreading and QA Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Defect detection | 25% | Finds seeded and independently observed defects |
| Source evidence | 20% | Identifies precise source location and expected content |
| Severity judgment | 10% | Rates impact consistently |
| Correction quality | 20% | Corrects defect without introducing another |
| Verification | 20% | Rechecks surrounding context and output |
| Error log quality | 5% | Complete, readable, actionable record |

Critical omissions affecting meaning or access should trigger remediation regardless of numeric score.
