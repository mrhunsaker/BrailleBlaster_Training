# General Practice Assignment Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Source analysis | 15% | Identifies structure, risks, and relevant specification |
| Workflow selection | 15% | Chooses defensible tool/process and explains why |
| Structural accuracy | 20% | Preserves hierarchy, relationships, and special structures |
| Translation/content accuracy | 20% | Output checked against source and applicable guidance |
| Quality assurance | 20% | Performs documented, systematic verification |
| Handoff/documentation | 10% | Files and decisions organized; limitations disclosed |

Suggested bands: 90–100 exemplary; 80–89 competent; 70–79 developing; below 70 requires targeted revision. Instructors may adjust weights for the learning objective.
