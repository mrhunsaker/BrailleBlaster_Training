# Demonstration: Proofreading and QA

1. Begin with an untouched source and a working output.
2. Choose a short representative passage and explain the proofing method.
3. Compare text, structure, translation decisions, and page-level production features.
4. Record a defect with location, evidence, severity, and proposed correction.
5. Make the correction.
6. Recheck the corrected area and adjacent content.
7. Close or assign the issue in the error log.

Emphasize that proofreading is a repeatable verification process, not a final visual glance.
