# Demonstration Planning Template

## Learning objective
What should learners be able to do independently?

## Source and starting state
Identify the exact source, version, and known defects.

## Instructor narration
1. State the problem and expected result.
2. Inspect source structure and requirements.
3. Explain the selected workflow and why.
4. Perform the operation.
5. Verify output against source.
6. Show one common failure and recovery.
7. Summarize transferable decision rules.

## Learner check
Ask learners to predict the next step or identify a defect before revealing the result.

## Accessibility check
Describe relevant interface actions and outcomes. Do not rely on color, pointer position, or screenshots alone.
