# Capstone Assessment Rubric

| Domain | Weight | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Intake and specification control | 10% | Source inventory, version control, requirements, clarification log |
| Source analysis and import strategy | 15% | Semantic review, conversion-risk analysis, documented workflow |
| Structural and content production | 20% | Correct hierarchy, lists, tables, figures, notes, and math handling |
| Translation and accessibility decisions | 15% | Evidence-based decisions and accessible alternatives |
| Pagination and production planning | 10% | Running heads, page numbering, TOC, volume plan, T-page considerations |
| Proofreading and quality assurance | 20% | Error log, source comparison, verified corrections |
| Handoff and documentation | 10% | Organized deliverables, sign-off, assumptions, unresolved issues |

## Performance descriptors
- **Exemplary (90–100):** Accurate, reproducible workflow; anticipates risks; verifies systematically; documents decisions and limitations.
- **Competent (80–89):** Meets core requirements with minor omissions that do not compromise meaning or access.
- **Developing (70–79):** Partial success; requires targeted correction or stronger verification.
- **Insufficient (below 70):** Material errors, missing evidence, or workflow gaps require resubmission.

## Critical-failure conditions
Require remediation regardless of total score if substantive content is omitted, meaning is materially changed, a known critical accessibility defect remains unresolved, or sign-off is claimed without required verification.
