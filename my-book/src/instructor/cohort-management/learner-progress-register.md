Use concise, objective language tied to observable criteria. Avoid sensitive personal details, diagnoses, speculative explanations, or subjective labels. Record accommodations and confidential support information only in the institution's designated restricted system, not here.

## Status definitions and escalation

- **Evidence submitted:** learner has supplied an artifact or demonstration for review; no attainment decision implied.
- **Review needed:** evidence has not yet been evaluated against the published criteria.
- **Meets criteria:** required observable evidence has been verified and recorded.
- **Further practice:** one or more criteria remain unmet; specify a concrete task and reassessment date.
- **Complete:** all required course gates have been met and the authorized reviewer has recorded the final decision.

Do not infer course completion from attendance or submission alone. Apply the assessment rubric and portfolio review process consistently.
