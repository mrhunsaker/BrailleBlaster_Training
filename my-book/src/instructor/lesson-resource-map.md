## Instructor planning notes

- Use A01–A03 as early reinforcement, not a substitute for initial demonstrations.
- Use A06–A09 after students have practiced the relevant import and structure tools.
- Use A10–A14 for transcriber-focused and specialized-content assignments.
- Use A15 as a practical assessment after proofreading instruction.
- Use A16 for a cumulative assessment or portfolio artifact.
- Use the Unit 5 resources selectively; do not treat all 22 Handbook lessons as prerequisites for the capstone.
- Keep answer keys separate from student distribution when assessment integrity matters.
