# Production Challenge: Instructor Evaluation Notes

A strong multi-volume plan:
- Uses actual pagination once a formatted draft exists.
- Places breaks at logical structural boundaries.
- Recalculates contents and cross-references after pagination changes.
- Documents running-head and page-number rules from the job specification.
- Accounts for figures, tables, glossary/index references, and preliminary pages.
- Includes proofing of representative pages and a final output check.

For the capstone, look for a traceable intake-to-handoff workflow. Students should not conceal assumptions or invent missing client requirements. They should flag ambiguity and request clarification where it affects meaning or production.
