# Contributors to this Curriculum {#contributors}

**Curriculum author:** Michael Ryan Hunsaker

This independent training resource was developed to support educators, braille transcribers, and learners using BrailleBlaster. The manuscript, lesson activities, and supplemental practice materials are released under the repository's stated license.

BrailleBlaster is developed by the American Printing House for the Blind (APH). This curriculum is an independent educational resource and is not an official APH publication.

Contributions, corrections, and issue reports should be submitted through the repository's GitHub project.
