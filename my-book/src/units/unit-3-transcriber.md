# Unit 3: Transcriber-Focused Lessons

Unit 3 follows a complete book through the production workflow. These are the tools that separate a good set of braille pages from a finished, professional braille volume: structured imports, correct page numbers, notes to the reader, a table of contents that matches the volumes, and transcriber-generated front matter.

## Lessons in this unit

20. [Importing and Working with NIMAS Files](../lessons/20-nimas-production-files.md)
21. [Running Heads and Page Numbering](../lessons/21-running-heads-and-page-numbers.md)
22. [Transcriber's Notes in the Text](../lessons/22-transcribers-notes.md)
23. [The TOC Tool and Distributing a TOC Among Volumes](../lessons/23-toc-and-volumes.md)
24. [The T-Page Generator](../lessons/24-t-page-generator.md)

Suggested total time: about 10.5 hours.

**Prerequisites:** Unit 1, plus Lessons 14 and 15 if you will work from NIMAS or other imported sources.

## The production sequence

The lessons follow the order you will actually work in:

1. **Import** the structured source and review it ([Lesson 20](../lessons/20-nimas-production-files.md)).
2. **Format** the body, and add running heads and page numbers ([Lesson 21](../lessons/21-running-heads-and-page-numbers.md)).
3. **Explain** your decisions to the reader with Transcriber's Notes ([Lesson 22](../lessons/22-transcribers-notes.md)).
4. **Divide into volumes** and build the table of contents ([Lesson 23](../lessons/23-toc-and-volumes.md)).
5. **Generate** the title page, Special Symbols page, and Transcriber's Notes page ([Lesson 24](../lessons/24-t-page-generator.md)).
6. **Proofread,** then emboss or export.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A generator or an automatic import saves time but never removes the need to proofread. Every lesson in this unit ends with verification.

## Unit outcomes

When you finish this unit you can:

- Import a NIMAS file, navigate it with the Book Tree, and decide what to do with production notes.
- Keep print and braille page numbers distinct and edit them deliberately.
- Decide whether a note is needed and where it belongs.
- Insert End of Volume markers before building and dispersing a TOC.
- Create transcriber-generated pages with the T-Page Generator and verify them.

## Unit 3 checkpoint

This checkpoint is a small version of the [Capstone](../capstone/capstone.md). It should take about two hours.

**The task.** Using a short multi-chapter source, plan and complete:

1. A running head and print page numbering for two chapters.
2. Two Transcriber's Notes: one inline and one as its own paragraph.
3. Volume breaks for a pretend three-volume set, chosen at chapter boundaries, with a TOC dispersed to the volumes.
4. A title page, Special Symbols page, and Transcriber's Notes page.
5. A one-paragraph production log explaining where you departed from the default.

**You are ready for the capstone** when your TOC and volume numbers agree and you have checked each generated page.
