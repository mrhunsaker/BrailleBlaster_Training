## Severity guide
- **Critical:** Meaning, data integrity, or required access is materially compromised.
- **Major:** Structure, navigation, or production requirements are incorrect.
- **Moderate:** A localized defect affects consistency or readability but not core meaning.
- **Minor:** Cosmetic or low-impact inconsistency.
