# Seeded Defect E01: Heading Hierarchy

## Student exercise

The source has two peer sections under the chapter heading. The working copy below has incorrectly formatted the second section as ordinary body text.

SOURCE
# Plant Care
## Watering
Water when the top layer is dry.
## Light
Most seedlings need bright indirect light.

WORKING COPY
# Plant Care
## Watering
Water when the top layer is dry.
Most seedlings need bright indirect light.

Identify the missing structure, correct the working copy, and verify navigation. Do not infer that bold or larger text alone creates a semantic heading.
