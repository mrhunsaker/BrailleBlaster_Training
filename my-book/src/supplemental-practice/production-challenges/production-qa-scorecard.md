# Production QA Scorecard

Use this scorecard for peer review or instructor assessment of a completed production challenge. Apply the job specification first; this scorecard does not replace required standards or agency-specific procedures.

## Rating scale

- **3 — Meets requirement:** complete, accurate, and supported by evidence.
- **2 — Minor revision:** substantially correct; limited correction needed.
- **1 — Major revision:** incomplete or unreliable; substantial rework needed.
- **0 — Not demonstrated:** missing, incorrect, or unsupported.

## Suggested interpretation

| Score | Interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 32–36 | Production-ready subject to required independent review |
| 26–31 | Strong work; targeted corrections required |
| 18–25 | Significant revisions and another QA pass required |
| 0–17 | Rework the workflow and resubmit |

A high score does not override a critical defect. Missing content, incorrect mathematical meaning, broken navigation, or an unresolved safety/accessibility issue must be corrected or escalated regardless of total.

## Critical-defect gate

- [ ] No known missing or duplicated source content.
- [ ] No known meaning-changing translation or formatting errors.
- [ ] Required navigation and volume references are verified.
- [ ] All critical defects are corrected and rechecked, or formally escalated.
- [ ] Final output has been inspected using the required proofing method.
