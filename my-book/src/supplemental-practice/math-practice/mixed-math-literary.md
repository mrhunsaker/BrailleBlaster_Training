# Mixed Literary and Mathematical Passage

## Student task
Mark each mathematical expression and identify where mathematical context starts and ends. Translate or recreate the passage using the lesson workflow. Compare output with source character by character.

## Passage
The class measured three garden beds. Bed A was 2 meters long. Bed B was 3 meters long. The total length was 2 + 3 = 5 meters.

The teacher wrote, “Add the lengths: 2 + 3.” The plus sign in this sentence is being discussed as a symbol, not used as an operation.

For a rectangle, area is calculated by multiplying length by width. If length is l and width is w, then A = lw. For a bed measuring 4 meters by 1.5 meters, the area is 4 × 1.5 = 6 square meters.

A student wrote the expression x + 2 in a notebook. Later, the student wrote, “The letter x is used as a variable.”

## Required analysis
1. Identify operational expressions.
2. Identify symbol/notation discussion in literary context.
3. Identify variables and units.
4. Explain how context will not extend into ordinary prose.
5. List checks used to confirm the translated result.
