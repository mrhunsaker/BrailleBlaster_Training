\section{Garden Measurements}
A rectangular bed is 3.5 meters long and 2 meters wide.

The area is \(A = l \times w\).

\[
A = 3.5 \times 2 = 7\text{ m}^2
\]

If the bed is divided into 4 equal sections, each section has area
\[
\frac{7}{4} = 1.75\text{ m}^2.
\]

Compare the expressions \(2(x+3)\) and \(2x+3\). Explain why they are not
equivalent for all values of \(x\).
