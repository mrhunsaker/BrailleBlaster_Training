# Field Guide: Seed Starting

## Before planting

### Materials

- Seed trays
- Growing medium
- Labels
- Watering container

### Prepare the trays

1. Clean each tray.
2. Add growing medium.
3. Moisten the medium without flooding it.

## After planting

1. Label each tray with:
   - Plant name
   - Date planted
   - Variety
2. Place trays in the designated area.
3. Record germination observations.

## Observation schedule

| Day | Observation |
|---|---|
| 1 | Record initial condition |
| 5 | Check for emergence |
| 10 | Record percentage emerged |
