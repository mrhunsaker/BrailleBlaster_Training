# Glossary and Index Production Sample

## Glossary
- **Germination:** The process by which a seed begins to grow.
- **Hardening off:** Gradually exposing indoor-grown plants to outdoor conditions.
- **Mulch:** Material placed on soil to conserve moisture and suppress weeds.
