# Community Garden Orientation

**Welcome**

The community garden has three work areas. Volunteers should sign in before beginning work.

## Safety

Wear gloves when handling soil or compost. Do not use tools that are damaged.

## Weekly tasks

1. Check the watering schedule.
2. Remove weeds from assigned beds.
   1. Place weeds in the green bin.
   2. Leave established plants undisturbed.
3. Return tools to the storage cabinet.

**Important:** Report a broken hose to the site coordinator.
