# Figure Decision Cases

For each case, choose a concise text description, tactile graphic, simplified representation, combined treatment, or documented omission. Consider instructional purpose, complexity, redundancy, and reader task.

| Case | Figure | Instructional purpose |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Decorative leaf motif in a chapter divider | No unique instructional information |
| G2 | Labeled parts of a flower | Identify and relate structures |
| G3 | Scatter plot with positive trend and one outlier | Interpret data and trend |
| G4 | Classroom photograph with no referenced details | General scene-setting |
| G5 | Circuit diagram with connected components and polarity | Trace a functional system |
| G6 | Map with route, legend, and three locations | Navigate and compare locations |
| G7 | Repeated icon beside each safety reminder | Reinforce a warning |

State what information must be preserved, what can be simplified, and what context the reader needs.
