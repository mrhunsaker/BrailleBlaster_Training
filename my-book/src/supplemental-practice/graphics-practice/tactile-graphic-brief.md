# Tactile Graphic Production Brief: Flower Structure

## Learning objective
Identify major parts of a flowering plant and understand their relative arrangement.

## Required content
- Root system below the soil line.
- Stem extending upward from the root system.
- Two leaves attached at different points on the stem.
- Flower at the top of the stem.
- Labels for root, stem, leaf, and flower.
- Clear soil line.

## Constraints
- Avoid texture that competes with structural information.
- Use distinguishable line/texture conventions and a legend where needed.
- Keep labels clear of the diagram and leader lines.
- Ensure the diagram is understandable through tactile exploration.
- Provide a short verbal orientation and text description.

## Student deliverables
1. Sketch or tactile layout plan.
2. Label and legend plan.
3. Description/orientation text.
4. Review checklist for collisions, ambiguity, and inaccessible distinctions.
